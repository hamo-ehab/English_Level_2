         Vocabulary: Illness & Symptoms
Allergy: A condition in which the immune system reacts abnormally to a foreign
substance, such as pollen, food, or pet dander.
Example: She has an allergy to peanuts, so she has to be very careful about what
she eats.




                                                                                  A
Asthma: A chronic respiratory condition characterized by difficulty in breathing
due to inflammation and narrowing of the airways.
Example: His asthma flares up during cold weather, so he always carries an
inhaler.

Cold (Common Cold): A viral infection that affects the upper respiratory tract,
causing symptoms such as a runny nose, sore throat, and coughing.
Example: I caught a cold after being out in the rain without an umbrella.
                            Vocabulary
Flu (Influenza): A contagious viral infection that affects the respiratory system, causing
symptoms such as fever, body aches, and fatigue.




                                                                                     A
Example: The flu season peaks during winter, and many people get vaccinated to avoid
getting sick.
Food Poisoning: An illness caused by consuming contaminated food or drink, often
resulting in nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea.
Example: She got food poisoning after eating undercooked chicken at a restaurant.
Heart Attack: A medical emergency that occurs when the blood supply to part of the
heart is blocked, often causing chest pain, shortness of breath, and other symptoms.
Example: He was rushed to the hospital after experiencing the symptoms of a heart
attack.
                                                                                 A
                                Vocabulary
Infection: The invasion of the body by harmful microorganisms, such as bacteria,
viruses, or fungi, often causing illness.
Example: The cut on his hand became red and swollen, indicating a possible
infection.
Stress: A state of mental or emotional strain or tension resulting from adverse or
demanding circumstances.
Example: She experiences a lot of stress at work, especially when dealing with
tight deadlines.
Cough: A sudden, forceful expulsion of air from the lungs, often as a reflex to clear
the airways of mucus or irritants.
Example: He developed a persistent cough after catching a cold
                              Vocabulary
Headache: A pain or discomfort in the head, scalp, or neck, often caused by
tension, stress, or other medical conditions.
Example: She took some aspirin to relieve her headache after a long day at
work.




                                                                                A
Pain: A physical sensation of discomfort, distress, or suffering, often caused by
injury or illness.
Example: He felt sharp pain in his leg after tripping on the stairs
Runny Nose: A condition in which mucus continuously flows from the nose,
often due to a cold, allergies, or an infection.
Example: Her runny nose got worse as the allergy season started.
                                Vocabulary
Sore Throat: Pain, irritation, or dryness in the throat, often caused by a viral infection, such
as the common cold or flu.
Example: He drank warm tea to soothe his sore throat.


Stomachache: Pain or discomfort in the stomach or abdominal area, often caused by
indigestion, infection, or other medical conditions.
Example: She had a stomachache after eating too much spicy food.
                              Vocabulary
Temperature (Referring to fever): A higher-than-normal body temperature, usually
indicating an illness or infection; commonly referred to as a fever.
Example: He stayed home from school because he had a high temperature and was
feeling weak.


Toothache: Pain in or around a tooth, often caused by decay, infection, or injury.
Example: She went to the dentist because she couldn't bear the toothache any longer.

        Vocabulary: Accidents & Journey
Injury: Damage or harm to the body caused by an accident, physical attack, or
other external force.
Example: She suffered a knee injury while playing soccer.
Bruise: A mark on the skin caused by blood trapped under the surface, often as a




                                                                             A
result of an impact or injury.
Example: He got a bruise on his arm after bumping into the table.
Burn: Injury caused by exposure to heat, electricity, chemicals, or radiation,
affecting the skin or deeper tissues.
Example: The burn on his arm was treated with cold water and bandaged.
Stings: A sharp, usually painful puncture of the skin caused by insects like bees
or wasps, or other sharp objects.
Example: She got stung by a bee while working in the garden.
                               Vocabulary
Cut: A break in the skin caused by a sharp object, resulting in bleeding.
Example: He got a small cut on his finger while chopping vegetables.
Bites: Wounds or punctures caused by an animal or insect biting the skin.




                                                                                    A
Example: Mosquito bites can be very itchy, especially during the summer.
Broke (as in "broken bone"): The fracture or break of a bone caused by injury or
stress.
Example: He broke his leg while skiing down the mountain.
Burnt (as in "burned"): Affected by heat, fire, or electricity, causing damage to
the skin or other materials.
Example: She burnt her hand while touching the hot stove.

              Vocabulary: Getting better
Antibiotics: A type of medicine used to treat infections caused by bacteria by killing or
stopping the growth of harmful microorganisms.
Example: The doctor prescribed antibiotics to help clear up her bacterial infection.
Cure: A treatment that completely eliminates a disease or medical condition, restoring
health.
Example: Researchers are working hard to find a cure for cancer
Prescription: Actions or measures taken to stop a disease or injury from occurring, or to
reduce the risk of it happening.
Example: He took the prescription to the pharmacy to get his medication for high blood
pressure.
Prevention: Actions or measures taken to stop a disease or injury from occurring, or to
reduce the risk of it happening.
Example: Regular exercise and a healthy diet are important for the prevention of heart
disease.
                               Vocabulary
Surgery: A medical procedure that involves the physical operation on a body to
treat an injury, disease, or deformity.




                                                                             A
Example: She underwent surgery to repair a torn ligament in her knee.
Treatment: Medical care provided to manage or cure a disease, injury, or
condition.
Example: His treatment for diabetes includes both medication and lifestyle
changes.
Vaccination: The administration of a vaccine to stimulate the immune system to
protect.
Example: Children are often given vaccinations to protect against diseases like
measles and polio.
