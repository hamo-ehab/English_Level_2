                     Vocabulary: Weather
 Blizzard: A severe snowstorm with high winds and low visibility.
 Example: The blizzard made it nearly impossible to drive, with snow piling up quickly on
 the roads.

Drought: A long period of dry weather when there is very little or no rain.
Example: Farmers are struggling to grow crops due to the ongoing drought in the region.


Flood: An overflow of a large amount of water beyond its normal limits, especially over
what is normally dry land.
Example: The river burst its banks after heavy rainfall, causing a flood that affected
hundreds of homes.
                     Vocabulary: Weather
Fog: A thick cloud of tiny water droplets suspended in the atmosphere, reducing visibility.
Example: The dense fog made it hard for drivers to see more than a few meters ahead.
Heat Wave: A prolonged period of excessively hot weather, which may be accompanied
by high humidity.
Example: During the heat wave, temperatures soared to record highs, and people were
advised to stay indoors
Hurricane: A type of tropical storm with very strong winds and rain, occurring in the
Atlantic and northeastern Pacific Oceans.
Example: The hurricane caused widespread destruction along the coastline, with winds
reaching over 120 miles per hour.
                      Vocabulary: Weather
Thunder and Lightning: Thunder is the sound caused by the rapid expansion of the
air surrounding a lightning bolt, while lightning is the flashes of light in the sky –
usually followed by thunder.

       Vocabulary: Environmental Issues
Air Quality: A measure of the cleanliness of the air, based on the amount of pollutants it
contains.
Example: The city monitors air quality daily to inform residents when pollution levels are
high.

Air Pollution: The presence of harmful or excessive quantities of substances in the air,
which can be harmful to human health and the environment.
Example: Factories and vehicles are major sources of air pollution in urban areas.

Noise Pollution: Harmful or excessive levels of noise in the environment, typically caused
by human activities like traffic, industry, and construction.
Example: Residents complained about noise pollution from the construction site next to
their apartment building.
        Vocabulary: Environmental Issues
Recycling: The process of converting waste materials into new materials and objects,
usually to conserve resources and reduce waste.
Example: Recycling paper, glass, and plastic helps reduce the amount of waste sent to
landfills.

Rubbish: Waste material; things that are no longer needed and are thrown away,
especially household waste.
Example: The park was littered with rubbish after the weekend festival.

Toxic Waste: Hazardous waste material that is harmful to humans, animals, or the
environment, often a byproduct of industrial processes.
Example: The factory was fined for improperly disposing of toxic waste, which polluted
the nearby river.
