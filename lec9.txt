Urban            Rural       Crowded
                    Vocabulary: Adjectives
Urban: Related to or characteristic of a city or town.
Example: The urban area is known for its skyscrapers and vibrant nightlife.


Rural: Relating to or characteristic of the countryside rather than the town.
Example: Many people enjoy the peaceful lifestyle of rural communities.


Crowded: Filled with too many people or things; not having enough space.
Example: The bus was so crowded that there was barely any room to stand.
                     Vocabulary: Adjectives
Local: Relating to a particular area, city, or a town; nearby.
Example: I prefer buying vegetables from the local market instead of the supermarket.


Noisy: Making or characterized by a lot of noise; loud or disruptive.
Example: The construction work outside made the street extremely noisy.


Safe: Protected from or not exposed to danger or risk; secure.
Opposite: Dangerous
Example: Make sure the children are safe when playing near the street.
                 Vocabulary: Adjectives
Near: Close in distance or time.
Opposite: Far
Example: The library is near my house.

               Vocabulary: Public Buildings
Tourist Information Centre: A place where tourists can get information, maps, and advice about
local attractions, events, and services.
Example: The tourist information center provided us with a detailed map of the city's historical
sites.


Retirement Home: A residential facility where elderly people live and may receive assistance
with daily activities if needed.
Example: My grandmother moved into a retirement home where she enjoys socializing with
other residents.


Town Hall: A building where local government officials work and where public meetings are
often held.
Example: The mayor addressed the community's concerns during a meeting at the town hall.
              Vocabulary: Public Buildings
Fire Station: A building where fire engines and firefighting equipment are kept, and where
firefighters work.
Example: The fire station is always prepared for emergencies in the neighborhood.


Community Centre: A place where members of a community can gather for social,
cultural, or recreational activities.
Example: The community center hosts free yoga classes every Wednesday evening.


Health Centre: A facility that provides medical and healthcare services to people in the
local area.
Example: I visited the health centre for my annual check-up.
              Vocabulary: Public Buildings
Concert Hall: A large building designed for live music performances and concerts.
Example: The orchestra's performance at the concert hall was breathtaking.


Leisure Centre: A place offering facilities for sports, fitness, and recreational activities.
Example: The leisure center has a swimming pool, a gym, and tennis courts.

                              Public speaking
 Making a speech is something which politicians do all the time and the
 ability to deliver a successful speech can make all the difference to a
 politician’s career. However, it is a skill which is difficult to teach and
 there are many people who find public speaking a terrifying experience.
 But making a speech or giving a presentation is something which most
 of us will have to perform at some point in our lives. And although
 there are very few people who are natural public speakers, there are a
 few things which the nervous speech-maker can do to make the
 experience easier.
